FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a residential solar installation take?

Most home installations are completed in one to a few days depending on system size, roof type, and whether battery backup is included. We'll confirm a timeline as part of your quote.

Do you provide a Certificate of Compliance (COC)?

Yes, every installation, solar or electrical, includes a Certificate of Compliance so your property stays insurable and compliant with South African regulations.

Can solar keep my home running during load shedding?

A hybrid inverter with battery storage can keep essential circuits running through outages. Tell us your must-keep-on appliances and we'll size a system accordingly.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?

Yes, we install and service residential and commercial systems, from single-home rooftops to 10kW+ commercial-scale installations.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homes and businesses across Gauteng. Get in touch to confirm availability in your area.
