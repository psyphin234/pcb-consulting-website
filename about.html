Our Mission

Reliable, Efficient, Sustainable Energy — for Everyone

PCB Consulting & Solar Solutions is committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and sustainable energy solutions for homes and businesses across South Africa. We bring solar installation and everyday electrical work together under one roof, so clients get consistent quality whether they're going off-grid or just need a plug fixed.

With load shedding and rising electricity costs a daily reality for South African homes and businesses, our goal is simple: make the switch to solar straightforward, and keep the lights on when the grid can't.
